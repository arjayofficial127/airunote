# Exam background music

Winter Lanterns is a 30-minute original procedural composition rendered with
synthesized bells and warm sine-based accompaniment. No Artlist recording,
third-party samples, or transcribed carol melody is used. The reproducible score
and renderer are in scripts/render-christmas-music.py (numpy + imageio-ffmpeg).
This describes provenance, not a guarantee of copyright registration or exclusivity.

The bundled MP3 is 80 BPM, stereo, 96 kbps and 30 minutes long. It fades in and
out, then loops in the exam. Existing Creator appearances use this track at 12%
volume unless an administrator saves different music settings. Other themes
default to music off. The live appearance gallery stays silent to prevent
multiple previews playing at once.

Appearance > Background music controls playback, audio URL, volume, title,
artist, source link, and credit visibility. Credits are hidden by default; administrators may show them. The bottom-left credit button only
shows information. Audible playback starts on the first pointer or keyboard
interaction; it is never assumed to bypass browser autoplay policy.

The Artlist track was removed from this implementation at the user's request.
Administrators substituting music must arrange the appropriate permissions.
No license purchase or rights certification is performed by these settings.
