The appearance preview keeps its isolated iframe so mobile breakpoints match the actual form. A ResizeObserver sizes the iframe to its content, including after screen/theme/font/image changes. Preview pages have no viewport-derived minimum height; this prevents height feedback loops. The editor page owns scrolling.

Respondents can pause the gift artwork. Full instructions remain unchanged in a separate reading area below the Creator header.
